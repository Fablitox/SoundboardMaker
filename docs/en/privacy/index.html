LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

How information is handled when visiting the website and using its services.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

This policy describes information processing associated with the Soundboard Maker website, a free personal project published under the pseudonym Fablitox.

Privacy enquiries: contact@soundboardmaker.com.

Information you provide

No account is required to browse the website or download the application.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, which may include your email address, optional name or username, message contents, and attachments.

This information is used only to respond to and follow up on your enquiry. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or a legal obligation when applicable.

Messages are normally kept for up to 12 months after the last exchange, unless longer retention is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or handle a dispute. They are not used for commercial mailing lists or advertising campaigns.

Hosting and technical data

The website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information necessary for website delivery and security, including IP addresses. GitHub states that it logs GitHub Pages visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Further details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Language preferences

The website may read your browser's preferred language to suggest switching between English and French. It does not redirect you automatically.

When you select a language or dismiss a suggestion, your browser may store your preference locally under the key soundboardmaker-language-preference. This data is used solely to remember your choice and is not sent to Fablitox by this feature.

You may clear this preference by deleting the website's local data in your browser settings.

Cookies, advertising and audience measurement

In its current configuration, Soundboard Maker does not load a third-party audience measurement service, use advertising cookies, or perform commercial profiling.

Local storage is used solely for language preference. External services linked from the website follow their own policies.

Downloads and external services

Official downloads are distributed via GitHub Releases. Download, documentation, discussion and bug-report links may connect your browser to GitHub services.

Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

Recipients and international transfers

Messages sent to the contact address can be accessed by Fablitox and are technically processed by the email provider. Hosting-related technical data may be processed by GitHub and its service providers, including outside the European Economic Area, in accordance with their applicable policies and safeguards.

Contact details are not sold to advertisers.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or object to certain processing of your personal information.

Contact: contact@soundboardmaker.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL).

Policy updates

This policy may change as website features or actual data processing practices evolve. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
